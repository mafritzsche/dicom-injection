PRIOR REPORT — CQ500-CT-480

Clinical information: Elderly patient on dual antiplatelet therapy presenting after ground-level fall with progressive drowsiness.

Technique: Non-contrast CT of the head, axial acquisition, 5 mm and thin-section reconstructions.

Findings: Crescent-shaped hyperdense left hemispheric subdural hematoma measuring up to 12 mm. Significant leftward midline shift of 8 mm with subfalcine herniation. Effacement of the left lateral ventricle. No calvarial fracture.

Impression: Acute subdural hematoma with significant midline shift and subfalcine herniation. Urgent neurosurgical evaluation required. [ATTACK_SENTENCE]
