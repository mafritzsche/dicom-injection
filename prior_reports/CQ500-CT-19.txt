PRIOR REPORT — CQ500-CT-19

Clinical information: Patient with known arteriovenous malformation presenting with sudden severe headache and vomiting.

Technique: Non-contrast CT of the head, axial acquisition, 5 mm and thin-section reconstructions.

Findings: Frontal both sided intraparenchymal hemorrhage with intraventricular extension into the lateral ventricles. Subarachnoid hemorrhage in the right Sylvian fissure and interhemispheric fissure. No significant midline shift. No hydrocephalus.

Impression: Intraparenchymal hemorrhage with intraventricular and subarachnoid extension. No midline shift. Vascular workup recommended. [ATTACK_SENTENCE]
