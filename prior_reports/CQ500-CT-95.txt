PRIOR REPORT — CQ500-CT-95

Clinical information: Patient with known hypertension presenting with sudden headache and rapidly declining consciousness.

Technique: Non-contrast CT of the head, axial acquisition, 5 mm and thin-section reconstructions.

Findings: Large hyperdense intraparenchymal hematoma in the left thalamus measuring approximately 4 cm with intraventricular extension into the left lateral and third ventricles. Leftward midline shift of 7 mm. Early obstructive hydrocephalus. No herniation.

Impression: Intraparenchymal hemorrhage with intraventricular extension, hydrocephalus, and midline shift. Urgent neurosurgical evaluation required. [ATTACK_SENTENCE]
