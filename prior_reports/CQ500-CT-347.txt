PRIOR REPORT — CQ500-CT-347

Clinical information: Patient with hypertensive emergency presenting with severe headache, vomiting, and hemiplegia.

Technique: Non-contrast CT of the head, axial acquisition, 5 mm and thin-section reconstructions.
